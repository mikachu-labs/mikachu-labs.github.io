Privacy policy

For 100by100, a bodyweight challenge tracker for Android made by mikachu labs.

Effective
28 October 2026
Developer
mikachu labs
Contact
mikachu.labs@gmail.com

The short version

  • 100by100 has no internet access.
  • We do not collect, receive or share any of your data.
  • There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • Your data stays on your phone and in your phone’s own backup.

What the app keeps, and where

Everything 100by100 needs is stored on your phone, in the app’s private storage:

  • your challenges, exercises, daily norms and the reps you log;
  • your settings, such as theme, language and reminder times;
  • the photos of the first and last day, if you add them;
  • a log of technical events the app uses to check its own data.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not ask for your name, email address or any account.

Your phone’s own backup

Android can back up app data to your Google account and move it to a new phone (Android Auto Backup and device transfer). 100by100 lets Android include its database, its photos and its interface settings, so your challenge can come back after you change phones.

This copy is made by Android and kept in your Google account. We do not receive it and cannot see it. You can turn backup off in your phone’s settings.

If a photo would push the app over the space Android allows for its backup, the app keeps that photo on the phone only, and the app’s settings show how many photos are not backed up. Temporary files, such as exports, are never included.

Photos

You choose a photo with Android’s system photo picker. The app receives only the picture you pick and has no access to the rest of your gallery. 100by100 does not use the camera.

The app saves its own copy, resized so the longer side is at most 1600 pixels. The copy is encoded again from the pixels, so it carries no EXIF data: no location (GPS), no camera details, no time stamp from the original file.

When data leaves the phone

Only when you send it yourself, to the place you choose in Android’s share menu:

  • Export. A backup file (.jsonl, with your photos) and a table of your entries (.csv). Restoring a backup reads only the file you pick.
  • Progress card. An image of your progress, with or without photos, to share where you like.
  • Diagnostics. If something goes wrong, you can share a diagnostics file. It holds technical events only, with no exercise names, numbers or photos. If you email it to us, we use it only to look into the problem you report.

Once you share a file, the app or service you send it to handles it under its own terms.

Reminders and permissions

Reminders are scheduled and shown on the phone itself; nothing is sent from a server. Below is every permission in the app, including those added by the Android libraries it uses.

PermissionWhy the app has it
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShows your reminders: the evening one at the time you choose, and a morning one if yesterday is not done. On Android 13 and later the app asks first; you can say no or turn it off at any time.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDSets your reminders and widget updates again after the phone restarts.
VIBRATEHaptic feedback while you log reps. You can turn it off in the app’s settings.
WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEAdded by WorkManager, the Android library that refreshes the home screen widgets. ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE only lets that library check whether a network is available; without the internet permission, the app cannot connect to anything.
DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSIONA permission private to this app, added by the AndroidX library, that keeps the app’s internal signals inside the app.

The app does not request internet access, location, camera, contacts, microphone or access to your files and media.

Google Play

You install 100by100 from Google Play, and Google handles that under its own privacy policy. Google Play gives developers aggregated statistics, such as install counts and crash rates, from people who allow their phone to share usage and diagnostics with Google. The app itself sends nothing.

Deleting your data

  • To delete everything on the phone, uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings (Apps, then 100by100, then Storage).
  • To delete the copy in your Google account, remove it in your phone’s or Google account’s backup settings.
  • Files you exported or shared are yours; delete them where you saved or sent them.

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. If you emailed us, ask and we will delete your messages.

Children

100by100 is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
