100by100

Bodyweight challenge tracker: log every day, keep the streak. Works offline.

Google Play Privacy policy

Not on Google Play yet. Android only.

How a challenge works

PartRule
Length30, 50 or 100 days, or your own from 7 to 365.
ExercisesUp to five, each with its own daily norm.
LoggingTap a step, type a whole set, or count rep by rep in focus mode. Widgets log a step from the home screen.
ReserveEvery 30 completed days earn one. It covers a missed day by itself; you can keep up to two.
RepairNo reserve left? Make the day up over the next two days at 1.5× the norm.
PerfectFinish without reserves, repairs, lowered norms or removed exercises.

Your data stays with you

  • Your entries live on your phone and in your phone’s own backup.
  • The app has no network access and no account.
  • Export a backup file and a CSV table whenever you want.
